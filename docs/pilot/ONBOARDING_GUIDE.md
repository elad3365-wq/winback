# WinBack — 1-page quick start for pilot businesses

**WinBack drafts follow-ups. You approve and send them. It never sends on its own.**

## First 15 minutes

1. **Sign up** at [pilot URL]/signup with your name, email and a password
   (8+ characters), or *Continue with Google*. If asked, confirm your email.
2. **Setup (3 steps):** business name, type, phone and location → how you
   follow up today → AI preferences (tone, whether you offer discounts or
   financing, business hours). You can change all of it later in **Settings**.
3. **Knowledge base** (left menu): add the facts the AI may use — e.g.
   "Water heater install: $1,400–$2,200", "Mon–Fri 8am–5pm", "1-year labor
   warranty". If it isn't here or in Settings, the AI won't promise it.
4. **Add your first leads:** **Leads → Add lead**. Only name, phone and
   service are required; add the estimate amount and a follow-up date if you
   have them.

## Every day

| You want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| See who needs a follow-up | **Dashboard** (follow-ups due, open value) or **Leads** (pipeline / list, filters) |
| Write a follow-up | Open the lead → **Generate AI Follow-up** |
| Check it | Read the yellow **Check before sending** notes — they flag prices, guarantees or time slots that aren't in your knowledge base |
| Change it | Edit the text directly, or **Regenerate**. **Discard** throws a draft away |
| Approve it | **Approve** locks the text |
| Send it | **Open in Messages** (opens your phone's texting app with the message) or **Copy** → send from your own phone/email → click **I sent it — mark as sent** |
| Record what happened | Change the lead's status (interested, call requested, recovered, lost…) and add notes — everything shows in the lead's timeline |

## Good to know

- **Several businesses?** **Businesses → New business**; switch from the top
  bar. Each business has its own leads, settings and knowledge base.
- **AI Control → Emergency stop** halts all automatic drafting at once.
  Drafts you ask for still work and are still reviewed by you.
- **Customer asked to stop?** Set the lead to *unsubscribed* — WinBack will
  refuse to draft anything else for them.
- **Email (Gmail)** is optional and not part of the first pilot week.
- Something wrong or confusing? Reply to the welcome email or text [phone].
