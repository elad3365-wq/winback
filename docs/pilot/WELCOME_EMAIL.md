# Welcome email — template (NOT SENT)

Send manually, one business at a time, only after the readiness gate in
`PILOT_PLAN.md` is green. Replace every `[…]`. Plain text works best.

---

**Subject:** You're in — your free WinBack pilot starts [day, date]

Hi [First name],

Thanks for applying to the WinBack pilot. You're one of five [industry]
businesses we're starting with, and I'd love to have you.

**What it is:** WinBack keeps track of every estimate you've sent and writes a
personal follow-up for the ones that went quiet. You read it, change anything
you like, approve it, and send it yourself. WinBack never messages your
customers on its own.

**The deal:** 14 days, free, no credit card. Nothing renews or charges at the
end. All I ask is that you try it on real open estimates and tell me honestly
what works and what doesn't.

**Getting started (about 15 minutes — or we do it together on a call):**

1. Create your account: [pilot URL]/signup
2. Answer the three setup questions about your business.
3. Go to **Knowledge base** and add what you actually quote — typical prices,
   hours, warranty wording. The AI only states facts you put there.
4. Go to **Leads → Add lead** and add 3–5 estimates you haven't heard back on
   (name, phone, service, amount).
5. Open a lead → **Generate AI Follow-up** → edit → **Approve** →
   **Open in Messages** (or Copy) → send → **I sent it — mark as sent**.

A 1-page guide is attached. I'd like to book 20 minutes on [date options] to
set it up with you — just reply with a time that suits you.

During the pilot, reply to this email or text me at [phone] for anything —
I usually answer the same day.

Thanks,
[Your name]
Founder, WinBack

P.S. Please don't put card numbers, passwords or health details in lead
notes. You can ask me to export or delete your data at any time.
